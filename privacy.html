TripHunters Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

TripHunters is a small travel content channel. The TripHunters publishing tool ("the app") is a private tool that the TripHunters owner uses to prepare photo and video posts for the TripHunters accounts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. This policy explains what the app does with data when it is connected to TikTok.

Who uses the app

Only the TripHunters owner uses the app, with the owner's own TikTok account. The app is not offered to the public and has no sign-up for other people.

What data the app receives from TikTok

  • When the owner connects the app with TikTok Login Kit: the account's open ID and display name, and an access token and refresh token that allow the app to send content to that account.
  • When the app sends a post: the status of that upload (for example "processing" or "failed").

The app does not read the account's videos, followers, messages or any other users' data.

How the data is used

The tokens are used only to send the owner's own prepared posts (pictures or video, a title and a description) to the owner's TikTok inbox, where the owner reviews and publishes them. The display name is shown to the owner so that it is clear which account is connected.

Where the data is stored

The tokens are stored on a private server that only the owner can reach. They are not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used for advertising or analytics.

Retention and deletion

The tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the app. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites, and can ask for all stored data to be deleted by contacting us at the address below.

Content in posts

Photos used in posts come from sources with a free licence (for example Wikimedia Commons) and are credited to their authors. Map images come from NASA imagery.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
