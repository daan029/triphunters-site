TripHunters Terms of Service

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. What this is

The TripHunters publishing tool ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the TripHunters travel channel. It prepares posts (pictures or video, a title and a description) and sends them to the owner's own TikTok account, where the owner reviews and publishes them. It is not a service for the public.

2. Use of the app

The app is used only by its owner, with accounts the owner controls. Every post is reviewed and published by the owner in the TikTok app. The app does not publish anything by itself.

3. TikTok

The app connects to TikTok through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API and follows the TikTok Developer Terms of Service and the TikTok Community Guidelines. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok account settings.

4. Content

Posts contain travel information that is checked against public sources and photographs that carry a free licence and are credited to their authors. The owner is responsible for what is published on the TripHunters accounts.

5. No warranty

The app is provided as it is, without any warranty. The owner may change or stop it at any time.
